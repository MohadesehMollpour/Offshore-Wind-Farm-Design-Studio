    Reading the chart

    Capacity factor is the electricity a farm actually delivered divided by what it would have delivered running flat out all year. The solid fill is the rolling 12 months to the end of . The dark line is the average since the farm was fully commissioned. A pale band means the last year fell short of that average.

    The dashed ring marks the UK offshore fleet over the same 12 months. Dome area is proportional to installed capacity.

    Wedges for projects that are still being built or are only consented have no generation record, so their cones are empty. No rolling 12-month figure is published for Lynn, Inner Dowsing or Scroby Sands, so only their lifetime average is drawn.

    Which sites are included

    Every UK site whose boundary centre lies within 150 km of the centre of Hornsea One (). East Anglia Three, about 154 km away, falls just outside. Hornsea Four was discontinued by Ørsted in 2025 and appears on the map only. Dogger Bank D has no published boundary and is not shown.

    Sea areas group neighbouring sites: the Hornsea zone, the Dogger Bank zone, the inshore Humber and Greater Wash sites, and the East Norfolk sites.
