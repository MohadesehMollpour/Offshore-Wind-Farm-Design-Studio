## Method, scope and gaps

Seven-view Lattice explorer with component anatomy, tracing/map, landscape/Sankey, disruption cases, local queries and ontology; separate revision-bound procurement evidence register.

Reference gaps include:

- Directory evidence is a contextual snapshot, not a project-specific procurement schedule. Obtain vendor quotations, qualification records and dated delivery commitments.
- vendors: missing; assign an owner and supply a source/review record.
- durations: missing; assign an owner and supply a source/review record.
- Screening calculations and user review records do not establish engineering, legal or operational approval.

Source/reference text entered in an evidence register is not an automatic API fetch or numeric-data import. Keep observed, modelled, assumed, contextual and unavailable values distinct. Received/reviewed records need a source and reviewed records an owner. Missing data must not become zero or approval.
