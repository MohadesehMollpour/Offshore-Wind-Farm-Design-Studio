## Management selection and digital twin

Management overview is first in desktop and mobile navigation, followed by **Wind Farm Digital Twin (3D View)**. The same name appears in the page heading and breadcrumb. Navigation retains the selected scenario ID in the URL. Initial loading honours that ID or the saved project selection; it no longer silently chooses a different revision according to the page's preferred dataset.

A live Three.js preview in Management overview uses the same scenario-bound farm-preview endpoint as the full twin. Its farm name, layout, turbine count and revision are explicit. Changing the region/layout matrix or snapshot selection changes both views. Other open tabs for this project receive the selection through the browser storage event. Back/forward navigation restores the corresponding revision, and reload retains the selected farm/layout.

The response includes site ID/name, jurisdiction, layout name, scenario ID/hash/revision and the computed geometry basis. Actual C23 computed positions take priority. When those do not exist, the geometry remains explicitly conceptual. Identical design parameters in different regional presets can legitimately produce identical geometry; the app does not invent surveyed farm coordinates or artificial differences between regions. Switching regions clears incompatible source bindings, AOI and reference point.

Layout 04 now supports a deterministic seeded feasible-point search when an AOI is supplied. Full rotor envelopes avoid the AOI boundary and user-declared exclusions; an indexed minimum-distance check enforces conservative isotropic spacing equal to the larger spacing assumption. Failure within a search budget is not reported as proof of global infeasibility. This is a geometry search, **not** an AEP/LCOE optimizer. Without an AOI, the twin remains conceptual.

## Category audit and implemented work

All 70 categories were reviewed and evaluated against the collected Hornsea evidence. The old generic “domain-specific transformation not yet implemented” fallback no longer appears in this scenario's results. This does not mean that every engineering package has sufficient evidence or approval.

The reference snapshot contains 22 source-backed screening views, 19 planning calculation views, 5 existing built-in calculation/project views, 20 evidence acquisition registers, an HSE risk register, a stakeholder register, and two geometry-blocked categories. The built-in project views include the audit, disclosure, dependency graph and reporting tools; their execution is not a scientific or regulatory approval. All remain subject to their listed evidence gaps.

The new **Work package readiness** page lists every category, its available view, evidence basis and outstanding investigations. It exports an audit CSV and evaluates all 70 packages using one shared scoped context. Overview coverage bars also distinguish available data/calculations from mere execution and use a pending-review colour where evidence remains unresolved. Counts distinguish populated tools from missing inputs; no blanket green completion/approval state is assigned. The structural workspace verifier now says it verifies package files, rather than claiming engineering completion.

New category-specific schemas cover 54 work packages. Existing source-backed charts stay available. The workspaces offer validated planning inputs, live draft previews, evidence/owner/source/review fields, notes and JSON import/export. Saving creates an immutable revision and recalculates its results. Draft calculations are labelled and do not modify saved results. Unknown numeric inputs remain blank. Received/reviewed evidence requires a reference; reviewed records require a responsible owner. No imported text or source URL is executed or automatically fetched.

### C61: financial sensitivity

C61 previously repeated the overview LCOE charts. It now calculates separate CAPEX, fixed OPEX, real discount-rate, wake-loss and commissioning-delay sensitivities. Selling-price sensitivity and a price/discount NPV response table become available when the user supplies a captured price in the same real price year. No electricity price, probability distribution or Monte Carlo confidence level is invented.

Price affects revenue and NPV, not cost-based LCOE. Commissioning delay holds CAPEX at year zero and shifts operating energy, revenue, operating costs and terminal removal, without inventing holding costs or a construction-finance model. The central LCOE remains 88.5504473 real-2024 GBP/MWh, consistent with the original overview case. A browser-only draft price of 100 real-2024 GBP/MWh produced a 5×5 response table and NPV of about £532.29 million; that example price was **not saved** as a project assumption.

### C62–C66 and later pages

C62 now contains an editable HAZID/control register and an interactive 1–5 ordinal likelihood/consequence matrix, with proposed residual scores kept separate. Six hazard templates start unassessed. A reviewed state requires initial scores, controls, owner and evidence. The initial six unassessed hazards and zero reviewed records remain the saved state. Browser test scores were drafts only. No matrix colour or score is a measured probability, tolerability decision, ALARP demonstration or approved emergency plan. Reference guidance: [HSE risk assessment](https://www.hse.gov.uk/simple-health-safety/risk/steps-needed-to-manage-risk.htm), [G+ offshore wind guidelines](https://gplusoffshorewind.com/work-programme/workstreams/guidelines), and [MCA offshore renewable navigation guidance](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/offshore-renewable-energy-installations-impact-on-shipping). These are reference materials rather than site approvals.

C63 calculates scheduled maintenance person-hours and direct-task FTE from explicit visit, task-hour, crew and productivity assumptions; it does not claim a whole-project staffing plan. C64 records stakeholder influence/interest, responsible owners and engagement evidence, without asserting or sending consultations. C65 computes an acyclic forward/backward critical path, activity float and a Gantt view from editable relative-month durations. The default template is 81 months; a draft grid-duration change to 84 months recalculated the finish to 105 months. Those durations are assumptions, not consent or supplier commitments. C66 calculates terminal-removal budget and discounted timing sensitivities using the declared cost basis, salvage and additional contingency; postponement examples do not imply legal permission to defer removal.

C68 now has disclosure/review coverage visuals alongside the existing contribution editor. C70 now shows report coverage and provides the current draft/technical-annex export rather than blank chart panels.

### Other data-supported calculations

C10 fits a descriptive positive-speed Weibull distribution by moments, retaining calm mass separately and rejecting a degenerate fit. C11 shows paired 10–100 m shear; it does not infer stability. C12 shows sample maxima/percentiles of hourly mean wind, not turbulence, gusts or 50-year design wind. C14 summarizes regional surface currents without inferring tide levels. C15 applies declared wind/wave perturbations to historical screening without claiming a climate forecast. C24 shows illustrative hub-point wake reductions, and C25 compares conceptual strategies at one declared wind condition without claiming optimization or validated annual yield. C28 keeps descriptive weather-year spread distinct from optional assumed-normal exceedance estimates.

C30 supports captured-price/curtailment what-ifs. C31 shows conceptual array strings, straight-line lengths and current assumptions; C32 provides three-phase and transformer-capacity screening; C33 needs an assumed/export-route length for an explicitly simplified resistive-loss calculation. These are not routed cable, protection, thermal or AC/DC designs. C36 records connection-time/cost scenarios without treating the TEC register as a connection offer. C40 requires user-supplied reservoir pore volume, efficiency and density before a volumetric storage assumption is calculated; no injectivity, containment or rights are inferred. C43 retains declared injection inventory/monitoring requirements. C45 shows nearby platform inventory without reuse certification; C46 compares declared logistics-use scenarios; C47 requires platform demand for an annual energy balance. C51 needs a declared life-cycle inventory for carbon intensity/payback. C52 uses published port distances for a straight-line transit proxy. C53 uses declared installation work rates and weather allowance, distinct from crew-transfer limits. C55 uses an explicit renewal availability approximation without changing the overview's energy-availability benchmark.

## Remaining evidence requirements
